## Rules (strongest evidence first)
- Open on a short number contrast that names the subject, with the payoff inside 4 s: 5 to 10 words, the number or the contrast first, never ending on the resolution or a bare absence. Evidence: bayern-atletico "Nineteen shots to nine. Atlético's unbeaten run: over." (9 words) 49.6% skipped, brighton-man city "Twenty-one shots. Zero goals." 51.4%, arsenal-liverpool "Seventeen shots. Zero on target." 54.7%, psg-villarreal (10 words) 56.6%, bayern-mainz "Twenty-six shots. Mainz managed one on target." (8 words, one measure, subject named) 52.0%, real-madrid-valencia "Madrid, two point two six xG. Valencia, point one six." (8 words, same measure for both named clubs) 53.9% and the best reel on views/holding/engagements; against tottenham-leeds "Sixty-one percent for Spurs. Sixteen shots for Leeds." (two measures split across the clubs, 62.2%), mun-bou (17 words) 73.9%, chelsea-liverpool (14 words, result last) 75.5%, man-utd-chelsea bare decimals 66.9%, real-madrid-racing "Then nothing." 67.6%, troyes-psg "PSG missed seven big chances. One goal won it." (abstract, ends on the result) 75.6%, man city-forest "Three league defeats in a row." (streak with no contrast) 62.6%. Confidence: high
- Keep reels to about 30 s; nobody reaches past the first ~8 s. Evidence: avg watch 6.5-7.7 s on the five reels of 28-40 s with watch ratio 0.18-0.27 (bayern-mainz 30.8 s: 8.6 s, 0.286, the best yet), against 3.6-5.8 s and 0.057-0.123 on the 47-63 s reels; arsenal-liverpool (28.7 s) 0.274, roma-man utd (31.4 s) 0.234, psg-villarreal (30.2 s) 0.218, bayern-atletico (40.4 s) 0.176. Confidence: medium
- Beat 1 is where viewers leave (4-12 s), in every v2 reel. Never spend it on a stat table that repeats the hook card; use it for the twist, the banter or the story. Evidence: psg-villarreal (5.7-10.6 s compare), roma-man utd (4.1-11.0 s compare), arsenal-liverpool (4.1-10.2 s compare), dortmund-bayern (5.7-12.7 s stat dump), real-madrid-racing (compare), magdeburg-bayern (4.3-11.5 s). Confidence: medium
- Put the twist or the banter right after the hook, as beat 1. Banter at beat 1 (5.5 s) was reached by the average viewer (7.0 s) on bayern-atletico; banter at 10.5 s on arsenal-liverpool was not. Whether banter wins comments or shares is still unproven (0 comments across 19 reels). tottenham-leeds had banter at beat 1 (5.4 s) but the average viewer left at 4.9 s, so the hook has to hold them first. Evidence: bayern-atletico, arsenal-liverpool, tottenham-leeds. Confidence: low
- Put the trend with `use_for: hook` in the hook (the streak, drought or milestone number), with the match twist second. roma-man utd opened on the opponent's pair ("Seven Roma shots before half-time. Zero after.", 57.7%) and left the nine-game winless run (the trend) to beat 2 at 11 s, after the average viewer (7.3 s) left. Evidence: 1 reel. Confidence: low
- Ask a two-name question with a side in the outro and the caption, and say it before the average viewer leaves if the reel is long. Evidence: only dortmund-bayern (6.1 shares+saves/1k reach, share rate) and psg-villarreal (5.7/1k, one save) got any shares/saves; both were reels with a fan_question; bayern-atletico asked "Kane or Laimer?" at 33 s and got 0. bayern-mainz asked "Karl or Kane?" at 24 s (average viewer left at 8.6 s): 0 shares, 0 comments. Zero comments on any reel so far, so the outro question has not worked yet. Confidence: low
- Don't re-read on the next beat what the hook card already shows (the score, the same stat). Evidence: magdeburg-bayern beat 1, psg-villarreal beat 1, roma-man utd beat 1. Confidence: medium
- Put the twist inside the hook as two matched numbers; "the real story at 12-18 s" never lands. Evidence: dortmund-bayern twist ("Bayern still drew") at ~12 s, real-madrid-racing twist (10 shots/3 goals before the 37th minute, 12/0 after) at 18 s, both after the average viewer left. Confidence: low
