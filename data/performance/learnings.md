# What works on @thescoutbrief
Updated 2026-11-22 · 29 reels reviewed (24 `match`, 5 `scout-files`) · confidence: high for match reels (15+), medium-low for Scout Files (5 reels, patterns still early)

The account is still small: 29 reels, 51-226 views each, two shares, three saves and **zero comments in total**. What we learn from the hook and from holding viewers is getting solid. What wins shares and comments is still unproven. Newest: fc-cologne-bayern (the first v3 reel) followed the hook rule and got the second-best skip rate (48.1%), but the average watch stayed at the median (6.32 s) because beat 1 opened with possession, not the Kane injury. Newest: leeds-chelsea (second v3 reel) repeated the same pattern: rule-following hook (55.1%), but beat 1 opened on possession and the average viewer left at 6.1 s, before the 1999 milestone (t1, `use_for: hook`) at 9.6 s. It still got 1 share and 2 likes from 106 reach (9.4 shares+saves/1k, best on the channel). Reach is falling on every reel: the last four posts reached 98, 97, 105 and 106, against a match median of 151. That is distribution, not content.

**Format v3 (2026-11-10): length target is now 28-35 s, max 40 s** (was 30-45, max 50). Write about 70-85 spoken words; the 110-word cap is not a target.

## Rules (strongest evidence first)
- Hook = the same measure for both named clubs, number first, 5-10 words, payoff inside 4 s. A paradox ending ("Still four-four") is fine only when both clubs are already named. Never end on a bare result or absence with no club named. Never split two different measures across the clubs. Evidence: villa-united "Twenty-six United shots. Twelve for Villa. Still four-four." 45.8% (best), cologne-bayern "Bayern one on target, Cologne four. Still one-one." 48.1%, bayern-atletico "Nineteen shots to nine..." 49.6%, brighton-city 51.4%, bayern-mainz 52.0%, real-madrid-valencia "Madrid, two point two six xG. Valencia, point one six." 53.9% (best reel overall), arsenal-liverpool 54.7%. Against: aek "Twenty-four shots to nine. Still trailing." (no club) 73.1%, troyes-psg (ends on the result) 75.6%, chelsea-liverpool (14 words, result last) 75.5%, mun-bou (17 words) 73.9%, man utd-chelsea (bare decimals) 66.9%, tottenham-leeds (two measures) 62.2%, chelsea-sunderland (one club, two measures) 60.9%. Confidence: high
- Keep reels at 28-35 s. The average viewer leaves at about 6-8 s whatever the length, so extra seconds only lower the watch ratio. Evidence: median watch 6.49 s on the 14 reels of 35 s or less against 6.29 s on the 5 of 35-45 s; watch ratio 0.210 against 0.175 (0.071 above 45 s); barcelona-atletico 41.7 s, 0.146; villa-united 34.7 s, 0.220; bayern-mainz 30.8 s, 0.286; valencia 33.5 s, 0.287. Now in format v3. Confidence: high
- Beat 1 (about 4-12 s) is where viewers leave in almost every reel. It must open on the reason behind the hook's paradox (injury, red card, tactical change, surprise_stat), or on the banter. Never another count of the hook's own measure, and never another measure of control (possession, passes, corners). Evidence: leeds-chelsea opened beat 1 with "More ball for Chelsea. xG…", and viewers left at 6.1 s, before the 1999 milestone at 9.6 s; cologne-bayern opened beat 1 with "seventy-two percent of the ball", and viewers left at 6.3 s, before the xG twist and before Kane's injury at 13.2 s; villa-united beat 1 = more shots (11 v 2), left at 7.5 s, before the 0.97 xG twist at 11.2 s; barcelona-atletico beat 1 = the hook's half-split again in xG, left at 6.0 s; valencia, psg-villarreal, roma-man utd, arsenal-liverpool and dortmund-bayern all lost viewers on a compare/stat beat that backed the hook. Confidence: high
- Don't re-read on the next beat what the hook card already shows (the score, the same stat, the same split in another unit). Evidence: barcelona-atletico (7/2 shots, then 1.37/0.15 xG), villa-united (26/12 shots, then 11/2 shots), magdeburg-bayern, psg-villarreal, roma-man utd. Confidence: medium
- Banter at beat 1 only helps if the hook held viewers and the banter is new information. Evidence: bayern-atletico (banter 5.5 s, average watch 7.0 s) and man city-forest (4.0 s / 6.4 s, ratio 0.201) were reached; tottenham-leeds (average watch 4.9 s), chelsea-sunderland (6.3 s, banter at 6.5 s) and troyes-psg were not; barcelona-atletico was reached (5.0 s / 6.0 s) but the banter repeated the hook and viewers left during it. Leeds-chelsea's banter (the 1999 milestone, 9.6 s) came after the average viewer left, yet the reel got 1 share and 2 likes (28.3/1k), the best value on the channel; with barcelona-atletico's 2 likes (13.3/1k) that is a weak sign that a verified rival-banter story is what people pass on, so move it earlier rather than drop it. Confidence: medium
- Ask the two-name question with a side by about 11 s, as beat 2, with a stat for each name. It never works at 24-33 s. Evidence: 0 comments in 24 match reels. Every outro question was asked after the average viewer left (leeds-chelsea "Leeds brilliant, or Chelsea awful?" 29 s; cologne-bayern "Missing Kane, or missing the target?" 29 s, even with the question as the caption's first line; bayern-atletico 33 s, bayern-mainz 24 s, valencia 29 s, villa-united "Lammens or Dalot" 30 s, barcelona-atletico 32 s). The only shares/saves came on leeds-chelsea (9.4/1k), dortmund-bayern (6.1/1k), psg-villarreal (5.7) and valencia (5.2). Confidence: low
- The fan_buzz angle has not yet beaten the data angles on comments or shares. Evidence: chelsea-sunderland, villa-united and barcelona-atletico: 0 comments, 0 shares+saves, median skip 60.9% (spread 45.8-62.5%) against 62.2% for the 19 other reels. Keep using it, but the hook rule matters more than the angle. Confidence: low
- Put the trend with `use_for: hook` in the hook, together with the score. Put `best_angle` (when it is a streak) in beat 1 or 2, never only in the outro. Evidence: leeds-chelsea left t1 (first Leeds win at the Bridge since 1999, `use_for: hook` and `best_angle`) to beat 2 at 9.6 s and never said the 1-4 score; the average viewer left at 6.1 s; cologne-bayern left t1 (Kane off at 20', `use_for: hook`) to beat 2 at 13.2 s, and the average viewer left at 6.3 s; roma-man utd left the nine-game winless run to 11 s (average watch 7.3 s); barcelona-atletico left t2 (two home defeats in a row, the best_angle) to the outro at 32 s. Confidence: medium

## Scout Files (player stories, Inspector Croak), judged only against Scout Files
- **New (#5 raphinha, 2026-11-21) — top Scout Files rule:** the spoken hook is a contradiction or open loop (what happened + why it's strange), never the full outcome. Keep "who else / how it ended" for beat 1. Raphinha's "Two red cards. Barcelona's Raphinha and Ancelotti, both sent off." told the whole story in 5 s: 64.6% skipped, average watch 3.67 s (left inside the hook, 0.05-5.25 s), ratio 0.09, 0 likes. The story's own open_loop ("He was already substituted. He still got a red card") was the better hook: "Substituted. Then sent off. Barcelona's Raphinha." The two best Scout Files hooks were both contradictions: dowman "Jude Bellingham's record? Gone. Max Dowman is sixteen." 52.6%, trafford "Spain, forty-three unbeaten? Over…" 57.7%. Closed statements did worse: ter-stegen bare fact 68.8%, raphinha 64.6%. Confidence: low (5 reels, consistent)
- **Freshness (#5):** Raphinha posted 6 days after the match (Japan 3-2 Brazil 14 Nov, posted 20 Nov; trends stale since 17 Nov). If the story is more than 3 days old, the hook needs a new angle (e.g. the 7-0 watched from the stands), or pick a fresher story. Confidence: low (1 reel)
- **On all 5 Scout Files the average viewer left before beat 1 ended (3.7-7.2 s).** Everything after about 10 s, including the question, is seen by a small minority. Fix the first 5 s before tuning the rest.
- **New (#4 wirtz, 2026-11-18):** beat 1 must be the present-day payoff, never backstory. Wirtz started beat 1 on its surprise word as asked, but the word opened a flashback ("Paraguay. This summer…"); viewers left at 5.74 s, the series' second-worst hold, and the Liverpool £116m angle (the audience's club) waited until 20 s. When the story is a national-team night, put the player's club and its number in the hook. Confidence: low
Only 3 reels so far (now 4), so these are early. Treat them as fixes and experiments, not proven rules.
- **Length: now 30-40 s, max 45** (`config/stories.json` → `duration_s`, changed 2026-11-17 from 40-55, max 60). About 75-95 spoken words, even though story-writer.md still says 110-140. The average viewer leaves at 4.7-7.2 s whatever the length, so the ratio rises as reels get shorter: ter-stegen 55.4 s 0.086, max-dowman 48.7 s 0.138, trafford 45.6 s 0.159. Ask the cliffhanger question by about 20 s (it came at 37-39 s on all three). Confidence: low-medium (3 reels, consistent)
- Beat 1 starts on its surprise word. No "Case file N." or the player's name read out at the top of beat 1; put those on the card kicker. Viewers left about 1 s into beat 1 on all three reels: trafford at 7.2 s ("Case file three. James Trafford:…", beat 1 from 6.3 s, the £31m payoff at ~11 s), dowman at 6.6 s ("Case file two. England under-twenty-ones'…", from 5.3 s), ter-stegen at 4.7 s (CV list). Confidence: low (3 reels, consistent)
- Beat 1 must add the most surprising fact NOT already in the hook, never the CV. Trafford's beat 1 was new (sold, then bought back for £31m) and got the series' best average watch (7.16 s, against 6.62 s for dowman, which restated the hook, and 4.71 s for ter-stegen, a CV list). Confidence: low
- Cold open = number/contrast + a famous name on frame 0, and say the player's name in the hook. Evidence: dowman "Jude Bellingham's record? Gone. Max Dowman is sixteen." 52.6% skipped; trafford "Spain, forty-three unbeaten? Over. Last save: City's former cup goalkeeper." (Spain named, Trafford only on the card kicker) 57.7%; ter-stegen bare number with no name 68.8%. Confidence: low (3 reels, consistent)
- Scout Files #1-#3 won 0 follows and 0 profile views (123, 131 and 106 reach), and 0 comments on all three outro questions. The series exists to win followers, so follows and profile views per 1k reach are its main score. Likes are its one bright spot (dowman 38.2/1k, trafford 18.9/1k against 6.2/1k for match reels).
