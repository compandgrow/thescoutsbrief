# What works on @thescoutbrief
Updated 2026-11-12 · 23 reels reviewed (22 `match`, 1 `scout-files`) · confidence: high for match reels (15+), low for Scout Files (1 reel)

The account is still small: 23 reels, 51-226 views each, one share, three saves and **zero comments in total**. What we learn from the hook and from holding viewers is getting solid. What wins shares and comments is still unproven. Newest: aston-villa-man utd has the best skip rate yet (45.8%); barcelona-atletico (62.5%, 0.146) ran 41.7 s.

**Format v3 (2026-11-10): length target is now 28-35 s, max 40 s** (was 30-45, max 50). Write about 70-85 spoken words; the 110-word cap is not a target.

## Rules (strongest evidence first)
- Hook = the same measure for both named clubs, number first, 5-10 words, payoff inside 4 s. A paradox ending ("Still four-four") is fine only when both clubs are already named. Never end on a bare result or absence with no club named. Never split two different measures across the clubs. Evidence: villa-united "Twenty-six United shots. Twelve for Villa. Still four-four." 45.8% (best), bayern-atletico "Nineteen shots to nine..." 49.6%, brighton-city 51.4%, bayern-mainz 52.0%, real-madrid-valencia "Madrid, two point two six xG. Valencia, point one six." 53.9% (best reel overall), arsenal-liverpool 54.7%. Against: aek "Twenty-four shots to nine. Still trailing." (no club) 73.1%, troyes-psg (ends on the result) 75.6%, chelsea-liverpool (14 words, result last) 75.5%, mun-bou (17 words) 73.9%, man utd-chelsea (bare decimals) 66.9%, tottenham-leeds (two measures) 62.2%, chelsea-sunderland (one club, two measures) 60.9%. Confidence: high
- Keep reels at 28-35 s. The average viewer leaves at about 6-8 s whatever the length, so extra seconds only lower the watch ratio. Evidence: median watch 6.49 s on the 14 reels of 35 s or less against 6.29 s on the 5 of 35-45 s; watch ratio 0.210 against 0.175 (0.071 above 45 s); barcelona-atletico 41.7 s, 0.146; villa-united 34.7 s, 0.220; bayern-mainz 30.8 s, 0.286; valencia 33.5 s, 0.287. Now in format v3. Confidence: high
- Beat 1 (about 4-12 s) is where viewers leave in almost every reel. It must bring something new: the twist, the reason behind the hook's paradox (surprise_stat), or the banter. Never another count of the hook's own measure. Evidence: villa-united beat 1 = more shots (11 v 2), left at 7.5 s, before the 0.97 xG twist at 11.2 s; barcelona-atletico beat 1 = the hook's half-split again in xG, left at 6.0 s; valencia, psg-villarreal, roma-man utd, arsenal-liverpool and dortmund-bayern all lost viewers on a compare/stat beat that backed the hook. Confidence: high
- Don't re-read on the next beat what the hook card already shows (the score, the same stat, the same split in another unit). Evidence: barcelona-atletico (7/2 shots, then 1.37/0.15 xG), villa-united (26/12 shots, then 11/2 shots), magdeburg-bayern, psg-villarreal, roma-man utd. Confidence: medium
- Banter at beat 1 only helps if the hook held viewers and the banter is new information. Evidence: bayern-atletico (banter 5.5 s, average watch 7.0 s) and man city-forest (4.0 s / 6.4 s, ratio 0.201) were reached; tottenham-leeds (average watch 4.9 s), chelsea-sunderland (6.3 s, banter at 6.5 s) and troyes-psg were not; barcelona-atletico was reached (5.0 s / 6.0 s) but the banter repeated the hook and viewers left during it. Barcelona-atletico's 2 likes (13.3/1k reach) are the only sign that banter wins likes. Confidence: medium
- Ask the two-name question with a side by about 11 s, as beat 2, with a stat for each name. It never works at 24-33 s. Evidence: 0 comments in 22 reels. Every outro question was asked after the average viewer left (bayern-atletico 33 s, bayern-mainz 24 s, valencia 29 s, villa-united "Lammens or Dalot" 30 s, barcelona-atletico 32 s). The only shares/saves came on dortmund-bayern (6.1/1k), psg-villarreal (5.7) and valencia (5.2). Confidence: low
- The fan_buzz angle has not yet beaten the data angles on comments or shares. Evidence: chelsea-sunderland, villa-united and barcelona-atletico: 0 comments, 0 shares+saves, median skip 60.9% (spread 45.8-62.5%) against 62.2% for the 19 other reels. Keep using it, but the hook rule matters more than the angle. Confidence: low
- Put the trend with `use_for: hook` in the hook. Put `best_angle` (when it is a streak) in beat 1 or 2, never only in the outro. Evidence: roma-man utd left the nine-game winless run to 11 s (average watch 7.3 s); barcelona-atletico left t2 (two home defeats in a row, the best_angle) to the outro at 32 s. Confidence: low

## Scout Files (player stories, Inspector Croak), judged only against Scout Files
Only 1 reel so far, so these are early. Treat them as fixes and experiments, not proven rules.
- Fix: the cold open must hold a contrast and name the player, not a bare number. Use praise vs number ("One of the world's best keepers. Forty-four caps.") or number vs number ("Forty-four caps. He could have had over a hundred."). Beat 1 explains the contrast; the CV (titles, clubs) never goes in beat 1. Evidence: ter-stegen hook "Forty-four caps when Neuer finally stepped away." with no name and no contrast: 68.8% skipped, average viewer left at 4.7 s just as beat 1 began listing "Seven Spanish titles…". The "over 100 caps" contrast came at 19.8 s. Confidence: low (1 reel, clear failure)
- Fix: aim for about 35 s, not 40-55. The average viewer left at 4.7 s of 55 s (watch ratio 0.086, 4th worst of 23 reels). Match reels show viewers leave at 6-8 s whatever the length. Cut to hook, why, twist, quote, question. Evidence: ter-stegen 55.4 s, 0.086; match reels at 35 s or less have a median ratio of 0.210. Confidence: low
- Scout Files #1 won 0 follows and 0 profile views from 123 reach. The series exists to win followers, so follows and profile views per 1k reach are its main score.
