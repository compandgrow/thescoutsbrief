## Rules (strongest evidence first)
- Hook = the same measure for both named clubs, number first, 5-10 words, payoff inside 4 s, and a paradox the final score makes surprising. In a draw or a win by the side with more of the ball, the paradox must be spoken (who had less of the ball, or fewer shots, yet made more), not left to the caption. Never end on a bare result or absence with no club named. Never split two different measures across the clubs. Evidence: psv-real-madrid "Won five-one. Out-shot eight to four at half-time." 43.8% (best), villa-united 45.8%, cologne-bayern 48.1%, celta-real-madrid 48.3%, united-sporting "Both Sporting goals started with United's keeper." 48.4% (no number, a clear culprit claim), roma-psg 48.4%, bayern-atletico 49.6%, brighton-city 51.4%, bayern-lille 51.6%, bayern-mainz 52.0%, valencia 53.9%, man utd-liverpool 54.2%. No-paradox hooks keep viewers through the hook but lose them right after: psg-nice (PSG won 3-2) 4.46 s; man utd-liverpool (4-0 v ten men) 6.17 s; roma-psg (big chances 4-2 in a 2-2, possession unsaid) 6.69 s, weakest v4 hold. Against: aek (no club) 73.1%, troyes-psg 75.6%, chelsea-liverpool (14 words) 75.5%, mun-bou (17 words) 73.9%, man utd-chelsea (bare decimals) 66.9%, tottenham-leeds (two measures) 62.2%, chelsea-sunderland 60.9%. Confidence: high
- Beat 1 (about 4-10 s) is where viewers leave in almost every reel: on all 4 v4 reels the average watch (6.69-10.85 s) fell inside it. It must say something new, in one sentence of at most 6 s (format v5): the reason behind the hook's paradox when that reason is itself a surprise; the counter-argument when the hook already names the culprit; otherwise the number that argues against the obvious reason. Never a replay of the hook in detail, never another count of the hook's measure, never possession, passes or corners. Evidence for: psv-real-madrid (three subs at half-time, shots on target 0 → 9) 10.85 s, ratio 0.319; bayern-lille (open-play xG 0.25 v 2.06) 8.51 s, 0.230; celta-real-madrid (Mbappé's missed penalty) 8.38 s, 0.246. Against: united-sporting retold the hook (save, penalty, dropped cross) 7.02 s, the referee twist waiting at 10.9 s; man utd-liverpool ("Yoro, sent off after eight minutes" closed the story) 6.17 s; leeds-chelsea and cologne-bayern (possession) 6.1-6.3 s; barcelona-atletico (hook's split again in xG) 6.0 s. Confidence: high for "never possession/re-count"; medium for "a surprising reason or counter" (3 for, 2 against); low for the 6-s limit (v5, under test)
- Keep reels at 28-35 s. The average viewer leaves at about 6-9 s whatever the length, so extra seconds only lower the watch ratio. Evidence: watch ratio 0.210 for reels of 35 s or less against 0.175 for 35-45 s (0.071 above 45 s); bayern-mainz 30.8 s, 0.286; valencia 33.5 s, 0.287; barcelona-atletico 41.7 s, 0.146. bayern-lille ran 37.5 s with a control-measure beat (210 final-third passes) that should have been cut. Confidence: high
- Put the trend with `use_for: hook` in the hook, together with the score; put `best_angle` (when it is a streak) in beat 1 or 2, never only in the outro. Evidence for: bayern-lille (t1, 30-game record, in the hook) 51.6% skip, 8.51 s on a favourite's defeat; psv-real-madrid. Against: leeds-chelsea left t1 (1999) to 9.6 s, avg watch 6.1 s; cologne-bayern left t1 (Kane off at 20') to 13.2 s, 6.3 s; roma-man utd left the winless run to 11 s, 7.3 s; man utd-liverpool used neither t1 nor the red-card theme in the hook. Confidence: medium
- When the favourite loses, tell it with one clean measure or a broken record, no gloating, injuries as context only. Evidence: bayern-lille 51.6% skip, 8.51 s and man utd-liverpool 54.2%, 6.17 s against man utd-chelsea (bare decimals) 66.9%, 4.92 s and mun-bou (17-word hook) 73.9%, 3.58 s. The difference is mostly the hook rule. Confidence: low-medium (2 for)
- Don't re-read on the next beat what the hook card already shows (the score, the same stat, the same split in another unit, or the same story in more detail). Evidence: united-sporting (hook "keeper", beat 1 the keeper's three moments), barcelona-atletico (7/2 shots, then 1.37/0.15 xG), villa-united (26/12 shots, then 11/2 shots), magdeburg-bayern, psg-villarreal, roma-man utd. Confidence: medium
- Ask the two-name question with a side as beat 2, starting by 10 s, with a stat for each name, built on the names the fan_buzz/headlines lead on, and repeat it as the outro and the caption's first line. Evidence: psv-real-madrid asked "Bellingham or Mbappé?" at 11.9 s (1.1 s after the average viewer left) and got the channel's first comment and 5 likes. The three 25 Nov reels asked at 10.9-11.8 s, 3-5 s after their average viewer left: 0 comments, 1 like in total. Questions at 15-33 s never got a comment (0 in 27 earlier match reels). Confidence: low (1 reel for)
- Banter at beat 1 only helps if the hook held viewers and the banter is new information. A verified rival-banter milestone is what people have passed on: leeds-chelsea (1999 milestone) got 1 share and 2 likes (28.3/1k), the best value on the channel, even though it came at 9.6 s. Move it earlier rather than drop it. Against: tottenham-leeds, chelsea-sunderland, troyes-psg (viewers gone before the banter); barcelona-atletico (banter repeated the hook). Confidence: medium

## Scout Files (player stories, Inspector Croak), judged only against Scout Files
- **Top Scout Files rule: the spoken hook is a contradiction or open loop (what happened + why it's strange), never the full outcome.** Evidence: bruno-fernandes "Player of the year. Then benched." 44.3% (best on the channel), dowman "Jude Bellingham's record? Gone. Max Dowman is sixteen." 52.6%, kane "Rejected by Arsenal, then the Ballon d'Or." 53.4%, trafford "Spain, forty-three unbeaten? Over…" 57.7%. Closed statements: raphinha "Two red cards… both sent off." 64.6% (left inside the hook, 3.67 s), ter-stegen bare fact 68.8%. Saying the player's name matters less than the contradiction: bruno's name was only on the card kicker. Confidence: medium (7 reels, consistent)
- **Beat 1 says the conflict, or the most surprising fact not in the hook, in one plain sentence of at most 12 words; quotes longer than 8 words go on the card.** Now `config/stories.json` → `structure[1]` (2026-11-23). On all 7 Scout Files the average viewer left before or during beat 1 (3.7-7.2 s). **A sentence that only rephrases the hook more mildly does not count as new:** kane's beat 1 "Even Spurs' youth coach didn't rate him top of his group." (after "Rejected by Arsenal") held 6.56 s, ratio 0.168, better than the story-2 beats it replaced but short of trafford, and lost viewers 1 s before the first real new fact ("loaned out four times", 7.5 s). Lead with the strongest new number or decision. Evidence: trafford (new fact: sold, then bought back for £31m) 7.16 s, the series' best; bruno (21-word tactical quote, the conflict never said plainly) 6.97 s, left 4 s into it; wirtz (flashback, "Paraguay. This summer…") 5.74 s; ter-stegen (CV list) 4.71 s. No "Case file N." or the player's name at the top of beat 1 (trafford, dowman). Confidence: low-medium
- **Freshness:** a story more than 3 days old needs a new angle, or pick a fresher one. Raphinha (6 days old) 64.6%; bruno (news from the day before) 44.3%. Confidence: low
