## Rules (strongest evidence first)
- Hook = the same measure for both named clubs, number first, 5-10 words, payoff inside 4 s. A paradox ending ("Still four-four") is fine only when both clubs are already named. Never end on a bare result or absence with no club named. Never split two different measures across the clubs. The twist must still be a surprise given the final score and the match events: if the side with fewer shots lost, or the loser had ten men from minute 8, a shots/chances hook has no paradox. Evidence: villa-united "Twenty-six United shots. Twelve for Villa. Still four-four." 45.8% (best match reel), cologne-bayern 48.1%, celta-real-madrid 48.3%, bayern-atletico 49.6%, brighton-city 51.4%, bayern-mainz 52.0%, real-madrid-valencia 53.9% (best reel overall), man utd-liverpool "Seven big chances for Liverpool. Zero for United." 54.2%, arsenal-liverpool 54.7%. No-paradox hooks kept viewers through the hook but lost them right after: psg-nice (PSG won 3-2) 4.46 s; man utd-liverpool (4-0 v ten men) 6.17 s. Against: aek (no club) 73.1%, troyes-psg (ends on the result) 75.6%, chelsea-liverpool (14 words) 75.5%, mun-bou (17 words) 73.9%, man utd-chelsea (bare decimals) 66.9%, tottenham-leeds (two measures) 62.2%, chelsea-sunderland 60.9%. Confidence: high
- Keep reels at 28-35 s. The average viewer leaves at about 6-8 s whatever the length, so extra seconds only lower the watch ratio. Evidence: watch ratio 0.210 for reels of 35 s or less against 0.175 for 35-45 s (0.071 above 45 s); bayern-mainz 30.8 s, 0.286; valencia 33.5 s, 0.287; barcelona-atletico 41.7 s, 0.146. In format v3/v4. Confidence: high
- Beat 1 (about 4-12 s) is where viewers leave in almost every reel. It must open a new question: the reason behind the hook's paradox only when that reason is itself surprising (a missed penalty, a star off injured), otherwise the number that argues against the obvious reason. Never another count of the hook's own measure, never possession, passes or corners. If the reason is obvious (an early red card), it belongs in the hook. Evidence for: celta-real-madrid beat 1 = "Five big chances missed. And Mbappé skied a penalty." held 8.38 s (ratio 0.246). Against: man utd-liverpool beat 1 = "The reason? Yoro, sent off after eight minutes." closed the hook, left at 6.17 s, before "shots were already three to one before the red" at 10.1 s; leeds-chelsea (possession, 6.1 s), cologne-bayern (possession, 6.3 s), villa-united (more shots, 7.5 s), barcelona-atletico (hook's split again in xG, 6.0 s). Confidence: high for "never possession/re-count"; medium for "the reason must be a surprise" (1 for, 1 against)
- Don't re-read on the next beat what the hook card already shows (the score, the same stat, the same split in another unit). Evidence: barcelona-atletico (7/2 shots, then 1.37/0.15 xG), villa-united (26/12 shots, then 11/2 shots), magdeburg-bayern, psg-villarreal, roma-man utd. Confidence: medium
- When the favourite loses, tell it with one clean measure and no gloating; it holds better than any earlier favourite_lost reel. Evidence: man utd-liverpool 54.2% skip, 6.17 s against man utd-chelsea (bare decimals) 66.9%, 4.92 s and mun-bou (17-word hook) 73.9%, 3.58 s. The difference is mostly the hook rule, so treat it as part of rule 1. Confidence: low
- Banter at beat 1 only helps if the hook held viewers and the banter is new information. A verified rival-banter milestone is what people have passed on: leeds-chelsea (1999 milestone) got 1 share and 2 likes (28.3/1k), the best value on the channel, even though it came at 9.6 s, after the average viewer left. Move it earlier rather than drop it. Against: tottenham-leeds, chelsea-sunderland, troyes-psg (viewers gone before the banter); barcelona-atletico (banter repeated the hook). Confidence: medium
- Put the trend with `use_for: hook` in the hook, together with the score; put `best_angle` (when it is a streak) in beat 1 or 2, never only in the outro. Evidence: leeds-chelsea left t1 (1999, `use_for: hook`) to 9.6 s, avg watch 6.1 s; cologne-bayern left t1 (Kane off at 20') to 13.2 s, 6.3 s; roma-man utd left the winless run to 11 s, 7.3 s; barcelona-atletico left t2 to the outro. Man utd-liverpool had t1 (`use_for: context`, hook_angle "Pochettino's first game: ten men by minute eight") and fan_buzz theme 1 (the red card, `use_for: hook + proof`) and used neither in the hook. Confidence: medium
- Ask the two-name question with a side early, as beat 2, with a stat for each name. Evidence: 0 comments in 27 match reels. Questions at 24-33 s were always after the average viewer left; the earliest so far, man utd-liverpool "Red card, or Liverpool?" at about 15.5 s (shots 3-1 before, 25-9 after), still came 9 s after the average viewer left and got 0 comments. The only shares/saves came on leeds-chelsea (9.4/1k), dortmund-bayern (6.1), psg-villarreal (5.7) and valencia (5.2). Confidence: low

## Scout Files (player stories, Inspector Croak), judged only against Scout Files
- **Top Scout Files rule: the spoken hook is a contradiction or open loop (what happened + why it's strange), never the full outcome.** Evidence: bruno-fernandes "Player of the year. Then benched." 44.3% (best on the channel), dowman "Jude Bellingham's record? Gone. Max Dowman is sixteen." 52.6%, kane "Rejected by Arsenal, then the Ballon d'Or." 53.4%, trafford "Spain, forty-three unbeaten? Over…" 57.7%. Closed statements: raphinha "Two red cards… both sent off." 64.6% (left inside the hook, 3.67 s), ter-stegen bare fact 68.8%. Saying the player's name matters less than the contradiction: bruno's name was only on the card kicker. Confidence: medium (7 reels, consistent)
- **Beat 1 says the conflict, or the most surprising fact not in the hook, in one plain sentence of at most 12 words; quotes longer than 8 words go on the card.** Now `config/stories.json` → `structure[1]` (2026-11-23). On all 7 Scout Files the average viewer left before or during beat 1 (3.7-7.2 s). **A sentence that only rephrases the hook more mildly does not count as new:** kane's beat 1 "Even Spurs' youth coach didn't rate him top of his group." (after "Rejected by Arsenal") held 6.56 s, ratio 0.168, better than the story-2 beats it replaced but short of trafford, and lost viewers 1 s before the first real new fact ("loaned out four times", 7.5 s). Lead with the strongest new number or decision. Evidence: trafford (new fact: sold, then bought back for £31m) 7.16 s, the series' best; bruno (21-word tactical quote, the conflict never said plainly) 6.97 s, left 4 s into it; wirtz (flashback, "Paraguay. This summer…") 5.74 s; ter-stegen (CV list) 4.71 s. No "Case file N." or the player's name at the top of beat 1 (trafford, dowman). Confidence: low-medium
- **Freshness:** a story more than 3 days old needs a new angle, or pick a fresher one. Raphinha (6 days old) 64.6%; bruno (news from the day before) 44.3%. Confidence: low
